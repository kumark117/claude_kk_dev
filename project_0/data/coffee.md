# Brewing coffee

## Pour-over

For pour-over coffee, use about 60 grams of ground coffee per litre of water, heated to roughly 92 to 96 degrees Celsius. Pour slowly in circles; the whole brew takes three to four minutes.

## French press

A French press uses a coarse grind. Steep the grounds for four minutes, then push the plunger down gently.

## Storing beans

Keep coffee beans in an airtight container, away from light and heat. Grind them just before brewing.
