# Brewing coffee

## Pour-over

Use about 60 grams of ground coffee for each litre of water. Pour water at
around 93 degrees Celsius, just off the boil, in slow circles.

## Grind size

Grind fine for espresso, medium for pour-over and coarse for a French press.
A sour cup usually means the grind is too coarse; a bitter cup, too fine.
